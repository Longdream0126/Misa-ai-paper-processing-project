Got up at 6:40 because the neighbor's dog decided the whole block needed a wake-up call. Made coffee, sat on the balcony, watched the recycling truck do its thing. Twenty minutes of nothing. Best part of the day, honestly.

Then the email I'd been dreading showed up. The client wants to "align on scope" again. Third time. I wrote back a short note saying sure, Tuesday works — and then spent forty minutes rewriting it so it didn't sound annoyed. It still sounds annoyed. Whatever.

At lunch I walked to the noodle place by the station. The owner has started putting up photos of his grandkids, all of them blurry. He asked if I wanted extra chili. I always want extra chili, but I said "a little" for some reason. Regretted it.

Afternoon was a blur of spreadsheets and a call where nobody had read the doc. You know that thing where everyone says "circling back" and nothing circles anywhere? Yeah.

Evening: tried to fix the bike light. Failed. Ordered a new one. Told myself I'd read for an hour instead and then fell asleep on the couch with the book on my chest. Woke up at 11, brushed my teeth, went back to bed. Wild Friday, I know.
